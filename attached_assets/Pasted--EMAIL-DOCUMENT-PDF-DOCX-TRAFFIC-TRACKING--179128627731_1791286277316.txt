--------------------------------------------------
EMAIL DOCUMENT / PDF / DOCX TRAFFIC TRACKING
--------------------------------------------------

The BLACK Trading website must support traffic tracking from
marketing documents and email campaigns.

I will send customers a DOCX/PDF promotional document by email.

The document may contain a clickable button such as:

"VISIT NOW"
"PURCHASE NOW"
"START YOUR $100K EVALUATION"

When the customer clicks the button, it should open the BLACK Trading website.

The button URL should support campaign tracking.

Example:

https://blacktrading.com/?utm_source=email&utm_medium=docx&utm_campaign=launch

or:

https://blacktrading.com/?utm_source=email&utm_medium=pdf&utm_campaign=100k_launch

--------------------------------------------------
VISITOR TRACKING
--------------------------------------------------

Track visitors using privacy-conscious analytics.

Record:

- Timestamp
- Landing page
- Campaign/source/medium
- Device type
- Browser type
- Approximate country/region where appropriate
- Referrer
- Session identifier

Do NOT collect unnecessary personal information.

Do not store raw passwords, payment credentials, private keys,
seed phrases, or sensitive financial information.

Respect applicable privacy/cookie laws and provide an appropriate
privacy notice/cookie controls where required.

--------------------------------------------------
IMPORTANT
--------------------------------------------------

Do not count every page refresh as a new customer.

Separate:

VISITS
UNIQUE VISITORS
REGISTERED USERS
CHECKOUTS
PURCHASES

A visitor is NOT automatically a customer.

A customer should only be counted after an actual successful/
verified purchase.

Also prevent obvious duplicate/bot traffic from making the
statistics meaningless where practical.
